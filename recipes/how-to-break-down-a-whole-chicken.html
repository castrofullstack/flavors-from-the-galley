How to Break Down a Whole Chicken

A step-by-step guide to fabricating a whole chicken into eight pieces. Keep your cutting board anchored with a damp paper towel underneath for a safe, stable workstation on smaller galley counters.

1. Remove the Legs (Thigh & Drumstick)

  • Place the chicken breast-side up with the legs facing you.
  • Pull one leg gently away from the body and use a sharp chef’s knife or boning knife to slice through the skin between the leg and the breast.
  • Bend the entire leg back and down toward the cutting board until the thigh bone pops out of its socket.
  • Cut cleanly through that joint, pulling the leg completely free. Repeat on the other side.

2. Separate the Drumsticks from the Thighs

  • Place the leg skin-side down. Look for the thin white line of fat running across the joint between the drumstick and the thigh.
  • Slice straight down along that fat line. If you hit the joint cleanly, the knife will glide right through without cutting bone. You now have two drumsticks and two thighs.

3. Remove the Wings

  • Pull a wing away from the body to expose the joint connecting it to the breast.
  • Cut through the joint to remove the wing intact. Repeat on the other side. You can trim off the wingtips to save for stock, or leave them attached.

4. Remove the Backbone

  • Stand the remaining carcass up vertically on the cutting board, resting on the neck end.
  • Cut downward through the ribcage on both sides of the backbone to separate the back from the breast crown.
  • Toss this backbone directly into your freezer bag—it is packed with collagen and perfect for your next 24-hour bone broth extraction.

5. Split the Breasts

  • Place the breast crown skin-side down on the board.
  • Score the cartilage running down the center, then press down firmly with the heel of your hand to crack the breastbone so it lays flat.
  • Slice directly down the center of the breastbone to separate the meat into two even, bone-in breast halves.

You now have your eight pieces: two drumsticks, two thighs, two wings, and two breasts.
