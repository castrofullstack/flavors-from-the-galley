Stoneware Chicken Pot Pie

Put leftover roasted chicken to work in a creamy vegetable filling tucked between two store-bought pie crusts.

Oven 425°F (220°C) Prep time 25 minutes Cook time 40 minutes Yield 1 9-inch pie

Ingredients

The Provisions

  • The Crust: 1 box pre-made pie dough (2 crusts, thawed), 1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp water (for egg wash and sealing)
  • The Meat: 2–3 cups leftover roasted chicken, shredded or cubed
  • The Produce: 1 medium russet potato (cut into ½-inch cubes), ⅓ cup chopped onion, ⅓ cup sliced carrots, ⅓ cup chopped celery, ½ cup frozen peas
  • The Base: ⅓ cup unsalted butter
  • The Liquids: 1¾ cups chicken broth, ½ cup milk or heavy cream
  • The Thickener: 2–3 tbsp instant mashed potato flakes (adjust as needed for thickness)
  • The Seasoning: ½ tsp dried thyme, salt and black pepper to taste

Preparation

Galley Log

  1. Par-bake the bottom crust. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Unroll the first pie crust and press it gently into your 9-inch ceramic pie plate. Prick the bottom gently with a fork a few times. Line the dough with parchment paper and fill it with pie weights (or dried beans). Bake for 10–12 minutes, until the edges are just set. Carefully remove the parchment and weights.
  2. Parboil the potatoes. Boil the ½-inch potato cubes in a small pot of salted water for 3–4 minutes, until they are just barely tender on the outside. Drain thoroughly and set aside to let the steam evaporate.
  3. Sauté the aromatics. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery. Cook for 5–7 minutes, until softened. Stir in the thyme, salt, and pepper.
  4. Simmer and thicken. Pour the chicken broth and milk or heavy cream into the skillet and bring to a gentle simmer. Remove from the heat and vigorously whisk in 2–3 tablespoons of instant mashed potato flakes. Let sit for 1 minute to hydrate and thicken into a hearty gravy.
  5. Assemble the filling. Fold the roasted chicken, frozen peas, and parboiled russet potatoes into the thickened sauce. Pour the mixture directly into the par-baked bottom crust.
  6. Seal and glaze. Brush the exposed rim of the par-baked bottom crust with a little egg wash. Drape the second crust over the filling, pressing the edges firmly onto the egg-washed rim to seal them together. Crimp the edges. Brush the entire top crust with the remaining egg wash for a glossy finish, and cut three or four small slits in the center to vent steam.
  7. Bake the pie. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until the top pastry is deep golden brown. If the edges darken too quickly, cover them loosely with a ring of aluminum foil.
  8. Rest before slicing. Let the pie rest at room temperature for 10–15 minutes before slicing so the gravy sets.
