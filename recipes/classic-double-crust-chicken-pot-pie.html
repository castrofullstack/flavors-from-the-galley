Classic Double-Crust Chicken Pot Pie

Put leftover roasted chicken to work in a creamy vegetable filling tucked between two store-bought pie crusts.

Oven 425°F (220°C) Bake time 30–35 minutes Rest time 10–15 minutes

Ingredients

The Provisions

  • 1 box pre-made pie dough (2 crusts, thawed according to package directions)
  • 2–3 cups leftover roasted chicken meat, shredded or cubed
  • ⅓ cup unsalted butter
  • ⅓ cup chopped onion
  • ⅓ cup sliced carrots
  • ⅓ cup chopped celery
  • ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1¾ cups chicken broth
  • ½ cup milk or heavy cream
  • ½ cup frozen peas
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Preparation

Galley Log

  1. Preheat the oven. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Soften the vegetables. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery and cook for 5–7 minutes, until softened.
  3. Cook the flour. Stir in the flour, salt, pepper, and thyme. Cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly to cook off the raw flour taste.
  4. Make the gravy. Gradually whisk in the broth and the milk or heavy cream. Simmer, stirring, for about 2 minutes until thick, glossy, and bubbly.
  5. Finish the filling. Remove the skillet from the heat and fold in the leftover roasted chicken and frozen peas.
  6. Line the pie plate. Unroll the first crust and gently press it into the bottom and up the sides of the pie plate.
  7. Fill the pie. Pour the chicken and vegetable filling into the bottom crust.
  8. Seal and crimp. Unroll the second crust and drape it over the filling. Fold the edge of the top crust under the edge of the bottom crust, then pinch to seal and crimp the rim.
  9. Vent the top. Cut three or four small slits in the center of the top crust to let steam escape.
  10. Bake the pie. Bake for 30–35 minutes, until the crust is deep golden brown and the filling bubbles through the slits. If the rim browns too quickly, loosely cover it with a ring of aluminum foil for the final 15 minutes.
  11. Rest before slicing. Let the pie rest at room temperature for 10–15 minutes so the gravy can set.
