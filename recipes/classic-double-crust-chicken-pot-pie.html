Classic Double-Crust Chicken Pot Pie

Put leftover roasted chicken to work in a creamy vegetable filling tucked between two store-bought pie crusts.

Oven 425°F (220°C) Par-bake 10–12 minutes Final bake 25–30 minutes Rest time 10–15 minutes

Ingredients

The Provisions

  • 1 box pre-made pie dough (contains 2 crusts, thawed)
  • 2–3 cups leftover roasted chicken meat, shredded or cubed
  • 1 medium russet potato, peeled and cut into ½-inch cubes
  • ⅓ cup unsalted butter
  • ⅓ cup chopped onion
  • ⅓ cup sliced carrots
  • ⅓ cup chopped celery
  • ⅓ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1¾ cups chicken broth
  • ½ cup milk or heavy cream
  • ½ cup frozen peas
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp water (for sealing and egg wash)

Preparation

Galley Log

  1. Preheat the oven. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Par-bake the bottom crust. Unroll the first pie crust and press it into your ceramic pie plate. Prick the bottom gently with a fork a few times. Line the dough with parchment paper and fill it with pie weights (or dried beans or uncooked rice). Bake for 10–12 minutes, until the edges are just set but not fully browned. Carefully remove the parchment and weights.
  3. Parboil the potato. Boil the ½-inch potato cubes in a small pot of salted water for 3–4 minutes, until just barely tender on the outside. Drain thoroughly and set aside.
  4. Soften the vegetables. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery and cook for 5–7 minutes, until softened.
  5. Cook the flour. Stir in the flour, salt, pepper, and thyme. Cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly.
  6. Make the gravy. Gradually whisk in the chicken broth and milk or heavy cream. Simmer and stir for about 2 minutes, until the sauce is thick and bubbly.
  7. Finish the filling. Remove the skillet from the heat and fold in the leftover roasted chicken, frozen peas, and parboiled potato.
  8. Fill the pie. Pour the filling directly into the par-baked bottom crust.
  9. Seal and crimp. Brush the exposed rim of the par-baked bottom crust with a little egg wash. Drape the second crust over the filling, pressing the edges firmly onto the egg-washed rim to seal them together. Crimp the edges as best as you can.
  10. Brush and vent the top. Brush the entire top crust with the remaining egg wash for a glossy finish. Cut three or four small slits in the center to vent steam.
  11. Bake the pie. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until the top crust is deep golden brown. If the edges darken too quickly, cover them loosely with a ring of aluminum foil.
  12. Rest before slicing. Let the pie rest at room temperature for 10–15 minutes before slicing so the gravy sets.
