Mary’s Carrot Cake

Spiced carrot cake with chopped peanuts or walnuts and a creamy cream cheese frosting.

Bake time 35 minutes Oven 350°F

Ingredients

What Goes In

Cake

  • 1½ cups salad oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 2½ cups flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1 teaspoon cloves
  • ½ cup chopped peanuts (or walnuts)
  • 2½ cups sugar
  • 5 tablespoons hot water
  • 1½ teaspoons baking powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1½ cups grated carrot
  • 4 egg whites, beaten

Frosting

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • ½ stick (¼ cup) butter, softened
  • 1½ cups confectionery (powdered) sugar

Preparation

Mix, Bake, Frost

Source note: The original card lists 4 eggs and 4 egg whites separately, and the first step mentions “eggs, yolks.” That wording is preserved below; the card does not clarify whether the eggs should be separated.

  1. Beat eggs, yolks, and sugar together until well blended. Add oil and hot water.
  2. Sift together flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, nutmeg, cloves, and cinnamon.
  3. Mix the dry ingredients into the wet mixture. Fold in grated carrots and chopped peanuts (or walnuts).
  4. In a separate bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff peaks form, then gently fold into the batter.
  5. Pour into a greased and floured cake pan or two 9-inch round pans.
  6. Bake at 350°F for 35 minutes.
  7. Let cool completely, then frost.

Frosting Instructions

Beat cream cheese and butter until smooth. Gradually add confectionery sugar and beat until creamy. Spread over the cooled cake.
