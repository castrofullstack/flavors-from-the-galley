Galley dough recipe

Pizza Dough

A hybrid dough for sourdough flavor with guaranteed lift from a little active dry yeast backup muscle.

Ready in Overnight Yields 2-3 pizzas Notes Hybrid sourdough / yeast lift

Ingredients

What Goes In

  • 500g Trader Joe's Unbleached AP Flour
  • 325g warm water
  • 50g young sourdough starter, from your pint
  • 1 tsp active dry yeast
  • 10g sea salt
  • 10g olive oil

Preparation

Mix, Knead, Cold Proof

  1. Mix: Combine the warm water, active dry yeast, and young sourdough starter. Stir in the flour, sea salt, and olive oil until a shaggy dough forms.
  2. Knead: Work the dough for 5-8 minutes, until smooth and elastic.
  3. Cold proof: Transfer the dough to an oiled container, cover, and refrigerate overnight.

Galley notes

Flavor First, Lift Guaranteed

  • A young starter brings mild sourdough tang without weighing the dough down.
  • The active dry yeast gives dependable rise, especially when the starter is still building strength.
  • The overnight cold proof develops flavor and makes the dough easier to stretch.
  • Let the dough warm slightly at room temperature before shaping for a more relaxed, even crust.
