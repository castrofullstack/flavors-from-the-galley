Wood Island Clam Chowder

Clean, Potent, Naturally Infused, and Herb-Enriched

Prep time 45 minutes (includes potato cooling) Cold infusion 1–24 hours (essential) Cook time About 25 minutes Yield 4–6 main course servings or up to 8 appetizer cups

Ingredients

The Provisions

  • 1 ¾ lbs Russet potatoes, peeled and cut into ½-inch dice
  • 2 cans (6.5 oz each) chopped clams, with juice
  • 2 cans (10 oz each) whole baby clams, strained with 1 cup brine reserved
  • 1 cup reserved clam brine from the whole baby clams
  • 1 large yellow onion, very finely chopped
  • 3–4 sprigs fresh thyme, tied with kitchen twine
  • 1 dried bay leaf
  • 2 ½ tsp table salt, divided (1 tsp for marinating and 1 ½ tsp for simmering)
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cups light cream (or 1 ½ cups heavy cream plus ½ cup whole milk)
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp instant mashed potato flakes, optional for thickening without flour
  • Fresh chives finely snipped, for serving
  • Oyster crackers for serving

Preparation

Galley Log

  1. Cook and Cool the Potatoes. Cover the diced potatoes with cold water by 1 inch. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to medium-low and simmer until al dente, about 3 minutes. The centers should offer slight resistance when pierced with a paring knife. Drain thoroughly in a colander and let cool completely in a bowl, about 30 minutes.
  2. The Cold Infusion. Mix the completely cooled potatoes with both cans of chopped clams and their juices, the finely chopped onion, 1 teaspoon salt, and 2 teaspoons black pepper. Transfer to a 1-gallon zip-top bag, seal tightly, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour and up to 24 hours. Chef’s note. Marinating off the heat lets the potatoes absorb the clam flavor and keeps the clams tender.
  3. The Herb Simmer Base. Strain the whole baby clams, reserving exactly 1 cup of brine. Set the whole clams aside. Empty the chilled potato mixture into a large pot and add the reserved brine, remaining 1 ½ teaspoons salt, tied thyme sprigs, and bay leaf. Bring to a bare simmer over medium heat, about 10 minutes, until tiny bubbles form around the edges. Reduce to medium-low and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring frequently, until the potatoes are nearly tender. Use tongs to remove the thyme and bay leaf.
  4. Finish with Cream & Clams. Stir in the whole baby clams and light cream. Bring the heat back toward medium and cook for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chowder is heated through and the potatoes are tender.
  5. Enrich & Thicken Off-Heat. Remove the pot from the heat. Stir in the unsalted butter until melted and glossy. For a thicker chowder, whisk in the instant mashed potato flakes until dissolved.
  6. Service. Ladle the hot chowder into heavy mugs or wide bowls. Top with freshly snipped chives and serve with oyster crackers.
