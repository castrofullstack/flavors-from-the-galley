Dutch Oven Lemon-Herb Chicken & White Beans

Golden chicken, creamy cannellini beans, and fresh spinach in a lemon-rosemary broth.

Chicken 1 whole bird · 3.5–4 lbs Oven 350°F (175°C) Bake time 35–40 minutes; check at 30
Galley technique: How to break down a whole chicken into 8 pieces →

The provisions

Ingredients

  • The Protein: 1 whole chicken (about 3.5–4 lbs), broken down into 8 pieces: 2 breasts, 2 thighs, 2 drumsticks, and 2 wings.
  • The Aromatics: 1 large yellow onion, diced; 2–3 large carrots, diced; 1 head of garlic, minced.
  • The Base: 1 quart homemade chicken broth; 2 cans (15 oz each) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed.
  • The Finishers: 1 bag fresh baby spinach; 1 large lemon, juiced; 3–4 fresh rosemary sprigs.
  • Pantry Staples: 1–2 tbsp olive oil; 1 tsp dried oregano; kosher salt and coarse black pepper.

Galley log

Preparation

  1. The Fabrication: Break the chicken down into 8 pieces. If the breast halves are exceptionally large, cut them in half crosswise so they cook evenly. Toss the backbone and wingtips into a freezer bag—they are the perfect foundation for your next 24-hour bone broth extraction.
  2. Preheat and Prep: Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Pat all 8 chicken pieces completely dry with paper towels to ensure a proper crust. Season generously on all sides with kosher salt, black pepper, and dried oregano.
  3. The Batch Sear: Heat the olive oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken skin-side down in two batches. If you overcrowd the pan, the temperature drops and the meat will steam instead of fry. Let them go undisturbed for 6 to 8 minutes until golden, flip for 2 minutes, then remove to a plate.
  4. Sauté the Aromatics: Lower the heat to medium. Drop the diced onions and carrots directly into the rendered chicken fat. Cook for 5–7 minutes until softened, then stir in the minced garlic for 1 minute.
  5. Deglaze the Pot: Pour in a splash of your homemade chicken broth and scrape up the fond (the browned bits) with a wooden spoon. Pour in the remaining broth.
  6. Strategic Assembly: Stir in the drained white beans, rosemary sprigs, and half the lemon juice. When adding the chicken back, submerge the dark meat (thighs and drumsticks) deep into the liquid. Rest the white meat (breasts and wings) right on top of the beans and dark meat, keeping their skin completely above the liquid line so they gently steam rather than boil.
  7. The Oven Bake: Cover and bake for 35 to 40 minutes. Because breasts cook faster than thighs, check the internal temperature of a breast piece at the 30-minute mark. Once the white meat reaches 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, away from bone, remove it to a clean cutting board while the dark meat finishes cooking. Check that all chicken pieces reach at least 165°F (74°C); continue baking as needed.
  8. The Final Fold: Remove the pot from the oven. Discard the rosemary stems. Fold in the fresh spinach by the handful until wilted (about 60 seconds). Squeeze the remaining lemon juice over the top, add the white meat back in if you removed it early, and serve.
