“Liquid Gold” Savory Quinoa

Toasted in beef tallow and simmered in rich bone broth.

Prep time 5 minutes Cook time 20 minutes Yield Variable (1:2 quinoa-to-broth ratio)
Original Recipe PDF

Ingredients

The Provisions

  • The Grain: Your remaining Sarita Organic White Quinoa
  • The Liquid: Homemade Beef Bone Broth (exactly double the volume of your quinoa)
  • The Fat: 1 tablespoon of rendered beef tallow (scooped from the top of your broth)
  • Optional Aromatics: Pinch of garlic powder, black pepper, or a small handful of diced onions

Preparation

Galley Log

  1. Rinse (Crucial Step): Measure your quinoa. Place it in a fine-mesh strainer and rinse it thoroughly under cold water for about 30 seconds to remove the bitter saponin coating. Shake off excess water.
  2. The Tallow Toast: Heat a saucepan over medium heat. Scoop about a tablespoon of the solid white tallow from your bone broth container and let it melt. Add the rinsed quinoa. Stir constantly for 2–3 minutes until it smells nutty and the moisture evaporates.
  3. The Liquid Gold Boil: Measure out your bone broth (2 parts broth for every 1 part quinoa) and add it to the toasted quinoa. Bring the pot to a rolling boil.
  4. The Simmer: Once boiling, immediately reduce the heat to the lowest setting. Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid and let it simmer undisturbed for 15 minutes.
  5. The Rest (Don’t Skip): After 15 minutes, turn off the heat completely, but do not open the lid. Let the pot sit on the warm stove for another 5 minutes to absorb the last bit of steam and prevent mushiness.
  6. Fluff & Serve: Remove the lid, fluff gently with a fork, and serve as an incredible base for your meals.
