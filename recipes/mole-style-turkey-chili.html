Mole-Style Turkey Chili

Ground turkey, lentil & sweet potato chili with a mole-style porter finish.

Prep time 20 minutes Cook time 1 hour Yields 8-10 servings

The provisions

What Goes In

The Fat

  • 2 tbsp Wagyu beef tallow

The Produce

  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 poblano pepper, diced
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced

The Sweet Potato

  • 1-2 sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • Splash of oil

The Meat

  • 2.5 lbs Farm to Family ground turkey

The Spices

  • 4 tbsp 365 chili powder
  • 1 tsp Badia jalapeño powder
  • 1 tbsp cumin
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper

The Liquids & Base

  • 1 can Mayflower Porter
  • 1 full can (6 oz) Hunt's tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp Better Than Bouillon Chili Base
  • 28 oz Cento San Marzano tomatoes
  • 2 cups bone broth

The Beans & Legumes

  • ½ cup dry lentils
  • 1 can black beans, rinsed
  • 1 can Great Northern beans, rinsed

The Mole Finish

  • 1 oz dark chocolate
  • 1 tbsp regular white sugar
  • 1 tbsp molasses

The Garnish

  • Fresh cilantro
  • Sour cream
  • Shredded white cheddar

Galley log

Preparation

  1. Roast the Sweet Potatoes: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss sweet potato cubes with oil, salt, and pepper. Roast on a baking sheet for 25 minutes until caramelized and fork-tender. Set aside to cool.
  2. Sauté the Produce: Melt the Wagyu beef tallow in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add the bell pepper, poblano, and onion. Sauté for 5-7 minutes until softened.
  3. Bloom the Spices & Pastes: Stir in the garlic, chili powder, jalapeño powder, cumin, and cinnamon. Add the full can of tomato paste, 1 tbsp Better Than Bouillon Chili Base, and 1 tbsp molasses. Cook for 1-2 minutes to toast the spices and caramelize the pastes.
  4. Brown the Turkey: Push the vegetables aside and add the ground turkey. Break it apart and cook until no longer pink, letting it absorb the spiced tallow.
  5. The Porter Deglaze: Pour in the Mayflower Porter. Scrape the bottom of the pot to release the savory browned bits (fond). Let the beer reduce by half (about 3-4 minutes).
  6. The Simmer: Hand-crush the San Marzano tomatoes into the pot. Add the dry lentils, beans, and bone broth. Bring to a gentle boil, then immediately reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for 45-60 minutes.
  7. The Mole Finish: In the final 5 minutes of the simmer, stir in the dark chocolate and white sugar. Let it melt completely into the chili, creating a glossy, deep, complex finish.
  8. Plating & Assembly: Ladle hot chili into bowls and top with the roasted sweet potatoes. Garnish each serving with a dollop of sour cream, shredded white cheddar, and fresh cilantro leaves. Mix any remaining sweet potatoes into the cooled leftover chili before storing.
